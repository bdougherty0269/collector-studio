The Collector — Official Character

Official visual reference

Ancient-looking alien with elongated skull, piercing green eyes, sinister toothy grin, aged textured skin, long skeletal fingers and dark clothing.

Personality: Intelligent, mysterious, sarcastic, darkly funny, occasionally terrifying.

Setting: Spaceship museum of bizarre artifacts and specimens, with mischievous alien assistants.

Abilities: Telekinesis and the theatrical illusion of reading the audience's minds.

Character appearance is locked as our reference. Creating a fully rigged 3D version is a future development phase.

🎙️ The Collector — Voice Lab

Free browser voice test. Uses voices installed on your phone/browser. Voice quality and availability vary; this does not yet create downloadable cinematic audio or imitate a particular actor.

Tap “Hear The Collector” to test his voice. For best results, try different installed English voices.

🎧 Cinematic Audio Workshop

Skip the robotic phone voice. Bring in a natural voice recording or an audio file you have permission to use. Play it here, then download a copy. Audio is not uploaded to GitHub or stored online.

Recording requires microphone permission and a supported browser. A recording is held in this page only until you download it; changing or closing the page clears it.

Official voice direction

Perfectly articulated neutral English. Smooth, measured, sophisticated and dryly sarcastic. The more threatening he becomes, the quieter and more polite he sounds. Never shout. Never imitate a specific actor.

Save and Transfer

Projects save automatically to this browser's local storage when you press Save or Add Scene. Browser data can be cleared, so download backups regularly.

This app is offline-capable when its files are stored locally. Opening it in Chrome from a local file or hosted website may behave differently across Android devices. No account or cloud synchronization is included yet.
